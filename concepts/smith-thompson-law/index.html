02 · The insurance side

We used to defend the insurance companies.

Every lawyer here has worked for insurers. We know how adjusters decide what a claim is worth, the tactics they use, and how courts read a policy. Now that experience works for you.

  • 01Insurers often reach out quickly after an accident with an offer that is far too low.

03 · How fees work

No fees until a settlement is reached.

Worrying about legal bills should not stop you from getting good advice. Here is how it works on contingency matters.

01

Free consultation

Sit down with a lawyer, in person, in a safe and private room. No obligation, and no pressure to go further.

02

We take it on

On injury claims and some employment matters, we offer contingency arrangements. We explain every stage and give you realistic expectations.

03

Paid from the settlement

We do not collect fees until a settlement is reached. Until then, you focus on recovering.

Wills, directives and real estate deals are quoted up front. Call us for pricing.
