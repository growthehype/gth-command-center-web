How we work

One project. Start to finish.

We only run one project at a time. That means fewer delays, steady progress, and someone on site who can solve the unexpected right away.

  1. 01

    Free site consultation

    We walk through your home, talk about what you want, and lay out your renovation options.

  2. 02

    A target budget and a clear scope

    You get a targeted renovation cost and a plan in writing, so nothing is a surprise.

  3. 03

    The contract that suits you

    Fixed price, cost plus, or design-build. Then we start as soon as possible.

  4. 04

    Hands on until it is done

    Immediate access to trades and labour, regular updates, and creative fixes on site when something unforeseen comes up.
