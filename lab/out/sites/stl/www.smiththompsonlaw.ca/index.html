Our Edmonton Legal Team Is Ready to Fight for You.

Practical Legal Solutions. Compassionate Advocacy. Proven Litigation Experience.

Legal problems rarely arrive at a convenient time. Whether you've been injured in an accident, wrongfully dismissed from your job, experienced workplace harassment, or are facing another serious legal dispute, the outcome can affect your health, your finances, and your future.

Our experienced and compassionated team of lawyers at Smith Thompson are committed to transforming the lives of our clients and will do everything necessary to help you and your family find resolution and compensation you deserve. We believe every client deserves experienced legal representation built on honesty, integrity, and genuine compassion.

We understand that every case is unique. There is no one-size-fits-all solution, which is why our legal team will take the time to listen closely to every detail, from the initial consultation through negotiation, mediation, or trial when necessary. Our focus is always on protecting your rights while reducing unnecessary stress throughout the legal process. We do not offer quick fixes or make unrealistic promises. Instead, we provide clear, honest guidance and a commitment to pursuing the best possible outcome—based on your unique circumstances.

Whether your matter involves a serious personal injury, an employment dispute, or complex civil litigation, you can count on Smith Thompson Law to stand beside you every step of the way.

We offer free, no obligation, consultations and offer contingency arrangements where we do not collect fees until a settlement is reached.
