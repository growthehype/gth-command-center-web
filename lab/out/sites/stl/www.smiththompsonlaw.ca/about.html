LITIGATION SERVICES

Motor Vehicle Accidents/ Pedestrian Accidents

Accidents involving motor vehicles can result in very serious injuries, even at low speeds. Insurance companies are often quick to reach out to injured motorists to expedite settlements at very low amounts. Thus, it is very important to consult with an experienced lawyer to determine fair compensation for your injuries. It is also important to wait until you know the full extent of your injuries and how they will impact your life before you agree to any settlement amount.

Slip and Fall Claims

In Alberta’s climate, slip and fall accidents occur frequently and can result in very serious injuries. These accidents typically occur in parking lots, outside of businesses or on residential and city sidewalks. The legal issues involved in a slip and fall claim are complex and require specialized legal knowledge and experience to properly navigate.

If you have experienced injury as a result of a fall, call our lawyers for a free consultation.

Fatalities

When personal injury result in a fatality, the results are tragic. Loved ones need to know that a fatality caused by a wrongful act or negligence can give rise to a legal action. The law recognizes that while money can never compensate for the loss of a family member, it can make up for the loss of financial support and services that the deceased would have provided had they lived.

If you have lost a family member, it is important to consult with a lawyer to determine what compensation you may be entitled to. The Fatal Accidents Act governs damages payable to family members who have suffered a loss. Compensation is available for bereavement, funeral and counseling expenses and loss of household services and income. Our lawyers can help you understand your rights to survivor’s benefits and any concurrent life insurance claims.

Professional Negligence

All professionals can make mistakes. When those mistakes cause you injury or cost you money, that professional’s insurance can respond to compensate you for your loss. Whether it be a mistake made by an accountant, a lawyer, an architect, engineer or other professional offering services, our lawyers can guide you through the process of obtaining fair compensation. Our lawyers have experience both in prosecuting and defending claims against professionals which gives our clients an advantage in navigating the complex legal issues involved in a professional negligence claim.

Mistakes made in the context of providing medical or dental care can have devastating consequences. These malpractice suits are often complicated, difficult cases which require an assertive lawyer who has experience dealing with medical issues and is not intimidated by the uncompromising position often taken by the Canadian Medical Protective Association (CMPA).

Employment Disputes/ Constructive & Wrongful Dismissal

If you are a long-time employee who has been dismissed without cause, you may be entitled to significant compensation. Our experienced lawyers can help you navigate the process of negotiating fair compensation taking into account your experience, age, level of responsibility and availability of alternative employment options. The lawyers at Smith Thompson Law offer contingency fee agreements in some circumstances.

A constructive dismissal occurs when an employer makes significant changes to the terms of the employment relationship or makes the work environment so hostile that the employee has no option but to terminate the employer-employee relationship. A determination of whether there has been a constructive dismissal is a complex and difficult area that requires the careful guidance of an experienced employment lawyer. It is crucial to consult with a lawyer prior to taking any steps to end the work relationship to ensure that actions are not viewed as a resignation rather than a constructive dismissal. We can help you strategically navigate this difficult time and advise you of the available options before making any important decisions with respect to your career.

Wills, Personal Directives and Enduring Powers of Attorney

An Enduring Power of Attorney is a legal document that allows a person of your choosing to manage your financial and legal affairs in the event you become mentally incapacitated. A Personal Directive is a legal document that provides direction to your family and health care providers as to your wishes when it comes to medical treatment in the event you become mentally incapacitated. These are extremely important documents that if not put in place in advance of your health complications, can unnecessarily burden your family with very expensive and lengthy legal obligations.

The lawyers at Smith Thompson Law can guide you through the process of ensuring you have a Will, a Personal Directive and an Enduring Power of Attorney in place so that you have the peace of mind that your affairs will be taken care of should anything happen to you.
