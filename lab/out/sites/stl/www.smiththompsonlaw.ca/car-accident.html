Types of Motor Vehicle Accident Cases We Handle

Motor vehicle collisions occur in many different ways, each presenting unique legal and insurance issues.

Our Edmonton accident lawyers regularly assist clients who have been injured in:

Car Accidents

From rear-end collisions to high-speed highway crashes, we help injured drivers and passengers recover compensation for their injuries and financial losses.

Commercial Vehicle Accidents (at work accidents)

Commercial vehicle collisions often involve multiple parties, commercial insurance policies, and complex investigations. We work to identify every responsible party.

Motorcycle Accidents

Motorcyclists frequently suffer catastrophic injuries due to limited protection. We advocate aggressively for riders injured because of another driver's negligence.

Pedestrian Accidents

Pedestrians are among the most vulnerable road users. Even low- speed collisions can cause life-changing injuries.

Bicycle Accidents

Cyclists deserve safe roads. When motorists fail to share the road responsibly, injured cyclists may have the right to seek compensation.

Hit-and-Run Accidents

Even when the at-fault driver cannot immediately be identified, there may still be options available through insurance coverage.

Multi-Vehicle Collisions

Determining liability in chain-reaction collisions often requires extensive investigation and experienced legal representation.

Dealing with Insurance Companies

Insurance adjusters often contact accident victims very soon after a collision.

While adjusters may appear helpful, their role is to evaluate claims on behalf of the insurance company. Before accepting a settlement, offer or providing detailed recorded statements, it is important to understand your legal rights.

Our lawyers manage communication with insurers so that you can focus on your recovery while we protect your interests throughout the claims process.

What Should You Do After a Car Accident?

The actions you take following an accident can affect both your recovery and your legal claim.

Whenever possible:

  • Seek medical attention immediately.
  • Report the accident as required.
  • Document the accident scene with photographs.
  • Gather witness information.
  • Keep records of medical treatment and expenses.
  • Avoid discussing fault publicly or on social media.
  • Contact an experienced accident lawyer before accepting any settlement.

Early legal advice can help preserve evidence and ensure important deadlines are not missed.
